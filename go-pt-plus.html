Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PTA compliance, SIM cards, and battery life.

Is this camera officially PTA approved in Pakistan?

Yes, 100% PTA Approved. All cameras imported and sold by S M Enterprises have officially cleared customs duties with lifetime DIRBS-compliant IMEIs on Jazz, Zong, Telenor, and Ufone networks.

How does the solar panel work on cloudy or rainy days?

The high-capacity 6000mAh lithium rechargeable battery holds enough energy to power the camera for up to 3 to 5 continuous days of overcast or rainy weather. Even diffused daylight will slowly trickle-charge the battery.

How much 4G SIM data does the camera consume per month?

For standard daily live checks and motion alarm notifications, a normal 5GB to 10GB monthly data package on Jazz or Zong is more than sufficient. The camera only consumes data when you view the live feed or when motion is recorded.

Can multiple family members watch the live stream together?

Yes! Up to 12 users can access the camera through the official Reolink smartphone app (iOS and Android) or Windows/Mac PC client simultaneously using secure admin credentials.
