PTA Approved 4G SIM Cameras in Pakistan: What You Must Know Before Buying

Updated August 2026 Verified by S M Enterprises PTA Regulations & Security

When purchasing a 4G LTE SIM-based security camera in Pakistan, the most critical factor to verify is its PTA (Pakistan Telecommunication Authority) approval status. Like smartphones, any device containing an IMEI number and a cellular SIM module is subject to DIRBS (Device Identification, Registration and Blocking System) regulations.

What Happens If You Buy a Non-PTA 4G Camera?

Many unauthorized importers and generic marketplaces sell cheap non-PTA 4G security cameras. While they might operate for the first 60 days, the PTA DIRBS system will automatically blacklist the IMEI number once the grace period ends. This leads to:

  • Permanent SIM Block: Your Jazz, Zong, Telenor, or Ufone SIM card will stop transmitting data.
  • Loss of Remote Access: You will no longer be able to watch live surveillance footage or receive intruder alerts on your phone.
  • High Heavy Individual Custom Taxes: Registering an individual non-approved IoT camera through PTA DRS portal can cost upwards of Rs 15,000 to Rs 25,000 in custom duties.
