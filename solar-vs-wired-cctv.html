2. Load-Shedding & Reliability Comparison

During power outages, traditional cameras rely on heavy UPS batteries that quickly degrade in Pakistan's high summer heat (45°C+). When the battery runs dry after 2 hours of load-shedding, your security is completely blind.

In contrast, the Reolink Go PT Plus charges continuously via its weatherproof monocrystalline solar panel and stores surplus power in its automotive-grade internal battery. It delivers up to 3 to 5 days of uninterrupted recording even during cloudy monsoons or heavy winter fog.

3. 1-Year Running Cost & Savings

Traditional setups run 24/7 on grid electricity and require fixed broadband bills (Rs 2,500/month). A Reolink 4G camera only uses data when motion is detected, running on a standard Rs 300 - Rs 500/month 4G SIM package.

Net 1-Year Financial Advantage: You save over Rs 26,400 in equipment, electricity, and broadband bills within the very first 12 months.
