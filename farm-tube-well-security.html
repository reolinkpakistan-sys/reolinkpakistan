August 2026 Agricultural Asset Protection Farm Tech

With the massive rise of solar-powered agricultural tube-wells across Punjab, Sindh, and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, solar panel theft, electric cable cutting, and motor vandalization have become major concerns for land-owners and zamindars.

Why Traditional Tube-Well Security Fails

Most agricultural tube-wells are situated far away from main village settlements in isolated fields. Hiring night watchmen (chokidars) is costly and often unreliable. Traditional CCTV cameras cannot be used because there are no internet landlines or stable round-the-clock grid electricity in open fields.
